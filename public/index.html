Help your little tree grow 🌱

Register for an event, collect food, then tap Just get it. Select the food type and enter the quantity you actually collected.

We estimate its weight using the food's unit. Your tree grows with the estimated grams of CO₂e avoided by your food pickups. Every 1500 g CO₂e completes a large tree and starts a new seed. All completed trees stay in your little forest.

  • Seed · in a pot: below 100 g CO₂e
  • Seedling · in a pot: 100–below 300 g CO₂e
  • Small tree · in a pot: 300–below 600 g CO₂e
  • Medium tree · in soil: 600–below 1000 g CO₂e
  • Large tree · in soil: 1000–below 1500 g CO₂e; at 1500 g a new seed starts

Your first availability report for each event waters your tree once. It has been running out also waters it, but adds no food weight. Drinks and registrations do not add growth weight. Editing a report does not count twice.

Sample pickups also contribute to tree growth during the demo.
